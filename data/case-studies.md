# Customer Case Studies and References

Reference customers require Customer Marketing approval before an introduction. Metrics
below are approved for external use unless marked internal-only.

## Northwind Financial — Regulated Rollout

Northwind Financial is a US retail bank with 2,400 seats on Enterprise+, live since
2024. They displaced Competitor X after their internal audit rejected a Security-only
SOC 2 report.

Approved metrics: a 34% reduction in time-to-close on customer escalations within two
quarters, 99.98% measured uptime over the trailing 12 months, and a documented $2.1M
annual reduction in manual reconciliation labor. Their audit prep time fell from six
weeks to nine days, which the CISO attributes primarily to the audit log export API.

Reference availability: two calls per quarter, security and IT audiences only. Their
CISO will not speak to pricing.

## Apex Logistics — TCO Displacement

Apex Logistics, 620 seats, moved from Competitor X in the 2025 renewal cycle after a
year-two price increase of 47%. This is the canonical TCO displacement story.

Approved metrics: 22% lower three-year total cost of ownership versus the Competitor X
renewal quote, implementation completed in 11 days against a 14-day plan, and 91% weekly
active usage at the 90-day mark. They took the $25,000 migration credit.

Internal-only: their initial discount was 26% off list, approved by the VP of Sales, and
they carry a $24,000 platform fee that was waived in year one on a three-year term.

## Meridian Health — HIPAA Deployment

Meridian Health operates 11 hospitals and runs 890 seats on Enterprise with a signed
BAA, deployed in the us-east region with customer-managed keys.

Approved metrics: 100% of PHI workflows migrated within one quarter, zero reportable
security incidents in 20 months of operation, and a 41% drop in clinician time spent on
administrative documentation. They completed their HIPAA security risk assessment with
no findings attributable to the platform.

This is the strongest reference for any healthcare deal. Note that they disabled the AI
Assistant org-wide as required for HIPAA scope — do not offer that feature in a
PHI-bearing deal without flagging the constraint.

## Aggregate ROI Metrics

Across 140 enterprise deployments surveyed in 2026: median payback period of 7.2 months,
average 3-year ROI of 287%, median 31% reduction in time spent on the primary workflow,
and a 94% gross revenue retention rate on Enterprise contracts.

The commissioned Forrester Total Economic Impact study (published March 2026) reports a
composite organization ROI of 244% over three years with a net present value of $4.7M.
Use the Forrester figure in RFPs; use the internal survey figures only in conversation,
and always label them as internal survey data rather than audited results.
