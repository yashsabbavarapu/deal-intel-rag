# Enterprise Pricing Guide

Effective 2026-07-01. Supersedes all prior sheets. Anything outside these bands needs
Deal Desk approval in advance — do not verbalize non-standard terms to a customer first.

## Seat Tiers and List Pricing

Pricing is per seat per month, billed annually in advance.

| Tier       | Seat band     | List (annual) | Floor with Deal Desk |
|------------|---------------|---------------|----------------------|
| Team       | 10-49         | $45           | $38                  |
| Growth     | 50-249        | $65           | $52                  |
| Enterprise | 250-999       | $88           | $68                  |
| Enterprise+| 1,000+        | Custom        | $54                  |

Seat counts are contracted minimums, not high-water marks. Overage above the contracted
band is billed monthly in arrears at list for that band, with no discount applied.
True-downs are not permitted mid-term.

## Platform Fee

Every Enterprise and Enterprise+ contract carries an annual platform fee that is
separate from seats and covers the dedicated tenant, SSO/SCIM infrastructure, audit
log retention, and the named Customer Success Manager.

The platform fee is $24,000 per year for Enterprise and $60,000 per year for
Enterprise+. It is waived for the first year on three-year contracts signed with
annual prepay. Team and Growth tiers have no platform fee. The platform fee is not
discountable below $18,000 under any circumstance.

## Minimum Commitments

The Enterprise minimum annual contract value is $75,000 including the platform fee.
Enterprise+ carries a $250,000 minimum. Below $75,000, route the deal to the Growth
tier — do not sell Enterprise features on a Growth contract as a one-off, because the
provisioning path does not support it.

Minimum contract term is 12 months. We sell 12, 24, and 36-month terms. Multi-year
discounts are 8% for 24 months and 14% for 36 months, applied to the seat line only,
never to the platform fee.

## Add-Ons

Advanced Analytics is $12 per seat per month. The Premium Support package (1-hour P1
response, 24/7 phone, named TAM) is 18% of the annual seat spend with a $30,000 floor.
Additional sandbox environments are $6,000 each per year. SAML SSO on the Growth tier
is $4 per seat per month; on Enterprise it is included.

Professional services implementation is quoted separately and typically runs $15,000
to $45,000 depending on data migration scope.

## Discount Authority

Account Executives may approve up to 10% off list without escalation. Regional Directors
may approve up to 20%. The VP of Sales may approve up to 30%. Anything beyond 30%, any
discount on the platform fee, or any payment term longer than net 45 requires CRO
approval and a documented competitive displacement or strategic-logo justification.

Quarterly end-of-quarter exceptions are not a standing policy. Do not promise a
better price "if they wait until the end of the quarter."
